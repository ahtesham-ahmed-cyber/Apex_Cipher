Editorial approach

What we do and do not publish

We would rather publish a small number of pieces we stand behind than a large number of thin posts. The same standard applies to this index.

Written by the people doing the work

Every article on this page reflects practice from engagements our team has worked on, not summary content or promotional messaging. Where an idea is uncertain or evolving, we say so rather than presenting it as settled.

Practice-based Openly uncertain

No client references

We do not name clients or describe specific engagements without permission, and nothing on this site does so.

No invented research

Where we reference industry findings, we link to the source. Where we offer an opinion, we label it as one.

Corrections are welcome

If something here is wrong or out of date, we would rather correct it than leave it standing. Email addresses for corrections are on the contact page.

No schedule commitment

We publish when we have something worth saying, not on a fixed cadence. This index is intentionally short, and that is the honest state of our writing rather than a content strategy.
