What each service involves

The actual work behind each capability

Below is a clear view of what we do, what we produce, and how each service fits into a broader engagement.

01 — Strategy

AI Consulting & Strategy

We help teams decide where AI is genuinely useful, where it is premature, and how to sequence investment responsibly.

Open AI consulting

Opportunity assessment

Where AI can plausibly help and where it cannot, based on the data and process reality you actually have.

Readiness review

Data quality, integration, governance, and team capability as preconditions for useful delivery.

Implementation planning

A realistic sequence of work with dependencies, decision points, and measurable checkpoints.

Responsible AI practices

Evaluation, traceability, and human oversight embedded into the plan rather than added at the end.

02 — Applied AI

Generative AI & Intelligent Agents

Systems that produce useful output against a defined task, with guardrails and evaluation appropriate to the consequences of being wrong.

Open AI solutions

Retrieval-augmented assistants

Grounded answers over your own content, with citation trails that let people verify what they receive.

Agent workflows

Multi-step tasks with clear boundaries, tool access scoped to the minimum required, and human checkpoints.

Evaluation harnesses

Task-specific test sets that measure quality change over time and catch regressions before users do.

Guardrails and observability

Input and output constraints, rate limits, and trace logging built into the system from the start.

03 — Engineering

Custom Software Development

Back ends, internal platforms, and integration layers designed so the teams who inherit them can extend them without fear.

Open software development

Domain modelling

Understanding your business vocabulary before choosing a data model or a service boundary.

Service and API design

Interfaces that are versioned, documented, and stable enough to build on.

Integration work

Connecting to the systems you already depend on — including the ones with awkward or undocumented interfaces.

Maintainable delivery

Tests, documentation, and review practices that make future changes routine.

04 — Platform

Cloud, Data & Platform Engineering

Container orchestration

Engagement models

Ways of working together

The right shape depends on the clarity of the problem, the internal capacity available, and the timeline you are working against.

Choosing a starting point

Most engagements begin with a short assessment, then transition into one of these models once the scope and constraints are clear.

Assessment

  • A short, focused piece of work that clarifies the problem.
  • Ends with a written recommendation and a proposed plan.
  • Useful when the direction is not yet settled.
  • No commitment to subsequent delivery work.

Delivery

  • A scoped build against an agreed architecture and outcomes.
  • Working software at the end of every increment.
  • Documentation and pairing built into the schedule.
  • Explicit handover and support-window planning.
