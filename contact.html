What happens next

A short process, in plain terms

This describes how a genuine enquiry would be handled. It does not describe what the form on this page does, because the form does not transmit anything.

  1. 01

    Read and reply

    A senior member of the team reads every enquiry. We respond with either a question, a proposed time to talk, or an honest decline.

  2. 02

    First conversation

    A working session, typically 45 minutes, focused on the problem rather than a presentation about us.

  3. 03

    Written follow-up

    A short written summary of what we understood, what we would need to know, and whether we recommend an assessment.

  4. 04

    You decide

    There is no follow-up sequence and no pressure. If it is not the right moment, the conversation can simply pause.
