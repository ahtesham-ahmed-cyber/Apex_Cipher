3. Information we collect

This website is a static frontend. It does not include analytics, session recording, tracking pixels, or third-party embeds that would collect information automatically. The only information this site could collect is what you choose to submit through a form or provide in an email. At the time of writing, the contact form on this site is a front-end demonstration: it validates input in the browser but does not transmit or store anything. See the contact page for the current state of that form.

If a form is later connected to a working backend, the categories of information it would collect, at minimum, are:

  • Identity and contact details. Name, email address, and optionally organization, submitted through a contact form.
  • Correspondence content. The message you write, including any attachments you choose to include.
  • Technical metadata. The standard technical data a web server receives, such as an IP address, a user agent string, and a timestamp.

We do not knowingly collect information about children, and we do not ask for special category data (such as health, biometric, or political information) through this website. If you believe such information has been provided inadvertently, contact us at PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL so it can be removed.

4. How information is used

Information submitted through this website would be used for the following purposes, and no others:

  • To respond to the enquiry you have submitted.
  • To keep a record of that correspondence for continuity of the relationship.
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations, where applicable to the nature of the enquiry.
  • To protect the security and integrity of our systems, for example by detecting obvious abuse of a form endpoint.

The lawful basis for that processing depends on the jurisdiction in which you are located and the applicable framework. Where a form is used, the basis would usually be your consent at the point of submission, the legitimate interest of responding to a direct enquiry, or a contractual necessity if a project discussion is already under way. The applicable basis should be confirmed with a qualified professional before publication.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use information submitted through this website for marketing to unrelated parties.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use cookies, and it does not store personal information in the browser. It does use the browser's built-in storage mechanism for two non-personal purposes:

  • Theme and state. Small flags that remember whether the mobile navigation panel is open, or whether a reveal animation has already played, for the current page view only.
  • Interaction preferences. Recognition of the user's reduced- motion preference, so decorative animation can be disabled as requested.

These values are not transmitted to any server and are cleared when the browser session ends, except for the reduced-motion preference, which is read from the browser rather than stored by the site.
