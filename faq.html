AI and technology

We do not begin from a provider. The model choice comes after the task, the source material, and the evaluation set are defined, and it is revisited as evaluation reveals what matters. This means we work with a range of providers and often with more than one, depending on what a specific task actually requires.

We define the task, the acceptable failure modes, and the human review points before choosing a model. Evaluation datasets, traceability, and clear escalation paths are part of the build, not a later compliance exercise. Where a task cannot be made measurable or cannot tolerate the failure modes the technology introduces, we say so and recommend a different approach.

We use AI tools the way most engineering teams do: for routine work where they clearly help, with human review. We do not use them to produce client-facing copy, architecture recommendations, or code that we have not reviewed line by line. The technology we sell and the technology we use are held to the same standard.

Wherever the constraints require. Cloud is often the right default, but some workloads cannot leave a specific environment for regulatory, latency, or cost reasons. We design for the environment you actually have, not the one that is fashionable.

Commercial

Assessment engagements are typically priced as a fixed fee, because their scope is defined and bounded. Delivery work is usually priced per phase, with the phase boundaries agreed up front. We do not quote for work we cannot scope, and we do not use hourly billing for anything larger than an assessment.

An initial conversation is always free, and an assessment can be small enough to fit comfortably within a modest budget. We do not take on work where the engagement overhead would exceed the value delivered — in that case, we would rather point you toward a more suitable approach than accept the project.

The code, documentation, and infrastructure definitions belong to the client. This is stated in the contract and is not conditional on any ongoing arrangement. Where we contribute open-source components, that is agreed in advance and handled transparently.
